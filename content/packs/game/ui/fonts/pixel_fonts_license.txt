Pixel Body and Pixel Small (pixel_body.ttf, pixel_small.ttf)
Converted from the X11 "misc-fixed" bitmap fonts 9x15 bold (Pixel Body) and 7x13 bold (Pixel Small),
ISO 8859-1 glyphs plus the horizontal ellipsis (U+2026), as pixel outlines in TrueType.
Source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/xorg/font/misc-misc

The misc-fixed fonts are in the public domain ("Public domain font. Share and enjoy.").
The conversions are released under the same terms.
