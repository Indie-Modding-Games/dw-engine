# gym

The town gym (`dwe::game::gym`, `game` layer): the choreography of each training course (`src/trn`:
`TRN_setup*Training` and `TRN_tick*Training`), the box that counts the gains and what the partner learns in the brains
course. The gains themselves belong to `packages/game/partner` (the ruleset's `training` section); what the
original did is in the [care sheet](../../reference/care.md#training).

It does not draw or touch the field: it depends on `core`, `assets` and `ruleset`. The numbers come from the ruleset's `gym` section
(`rules/<id>/gym.yaml`, schema `content/schemas/ruleset_gym.schema.json`), in frames of the original.
The story opens a course with routines 39 (`loadTrainingLibrary`) and 7, and waits for it to end; in `apps/game`
`StoryGym` does it over the `GymScreen`.

## Usage

```cpp
auto rules = dwe::game::gym::readGymRules(ruleset);       // the gym section; without it, no course
const auto* course = rules->courseOfChoice(map, choice);   // the map and variable 254 choose the course
dwe::game::gym::CourseRun run{*rules, *course, withSlots};   // variable 246 sets the slot machine
run.tick(ports, seconds, frameSeconds, {.confirm = pressed, .cancel = pressed, .cancelHeld = held});
if (run.finished()) { ... }                                // TRAINING_COMPLETE: the script continues
```

- **Sites** (`sites` in `gym.yaml`): each map with courses says its gains place (`trn` or `trn2`) and whether it counts
  practice. The town gym (0x70) has all six; the other maps, one or two: 0x4e (offense and speed) and
  0x77 (brains with a teacher) of the first module, and 0x63, 0x6b, 0x6c and 0xa5 of the second (`src/trn2`).
- **Courses** (`CourseRules`): what is common (station, camera, session duration, rest, sounds and animations) and each one's own
  choreography (`CourseDetail`): HP (the log that hits), MP (the waterfall), offense (the punching bag, or the
  hit that swaps map objects), defense (the swinging log, the guard with hits or the falling object),
  speed (the run, or the treadmill) and brains (the blackboard with the Tamer or with a teacher).
- **Execution** (`CourseRun`): advances whole frames of the original with the states numbered like each course's
  `TRAINING_STATE`. The session ends when its duration is met or on cancel; then the stats box
  (`StatCountup`) counts the gains, one point per frame, and confirm (Enter or Space) or cancel (Ctrl or Backspace) finishes it at once.
- **Slot machine** (`SlotMachine`, `trn_slots.c`): with variable 246 at 1, the slot machine decides when the session ends and the
  pace of the gains in tenths: the player stops three reels and the line pays more with three sevens or with three of the
  course's symbol (`slot_symbol`). A hidden help, drawn per session, brings the stop closer to a pair or to the seven
  (or pushes the last reel past a near miss). The `slots` section has the reels, the helps, the
  payouts and the sounds.
- **Ports** (`GymPorts`): everything the course asks of the game (walking, animating, moving map objects, clouds,
  particles, sounds, camera and the reward). What is common over the field and the game is
  `session::GymFieldPorts` ([session](../apps/session.md#gym-in-the-field-gymfieldports)); `apps/game`
  (`GymScreen`) adds what is drawn.
  Since the story holds the field while it waits for the gym, `GymScreen` moves the actors
  like a script: they walk to the station's square at their clip's speed, turn, animate and the
  camera moves to the station in the course's frames. The clouds and
  sparks are drawn by `FieldEffects` (`apps/game/src/field_effects.cpp`).
- **Brains** (`brain_learning.hpp`): `brainMilestones` says which new orders the gain opens and whether it is time to
  try a move (`TRN_tryLearnMove`); `trainingMove` chooses the move by the species' specials and
  their weights.
