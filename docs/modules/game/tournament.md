# tournament

The arena tournaments (`dwe::game::tournament`, `game` layer): the rivals a cup draws, the bracket of eight with the
matches the player does not play decided by level, each rival's stats and techniques, and how many wins take the cup.
It draws nothing and does not play the matches: it depends on `core`, `assets` and `ruleset`. The numbers come from the
`tournament` section of the ruleset (`rules/<id>/tournament.yaml`) and from `data/stadium` (the original's tables: the
rival's base value per cup and the win chance per level), which `dwx` extracts.

## Usage

```cpp
auto rules = dwe::game::tournament::parseTournamentRules(*ruleset.section("tournament"), stadium);
auto cup = dwe::game::tournament::cupDataOf(listing, cupNumber);      // section 10 of the map script
auto rivals = dwe::game::tournament::drawRivals(*rules, cupNumber, *cup, resident, partnerSpecies, random);
auto bracket = dwe::game::tournament::drawBracket(*rules, *rivals, partnerSpecies, levelOf, random);
auto rival = dwe::game::tournament::makeRival(*rules, cupNumber, species, random);
```

- **Start**: script routine 6 (`call_routine(6)`) opens the tournament screen; `session::TournamentAdapter` reads the
  cup (variable 3), the cup's lists in the active map's script and the city's residents (triggers 200 + species), and
  draws rivals and bracket.
- **Registration**: script routine 18 (`call_routine(18)`, the lobby receptionist) opens
  `apps/game/src/tournament_schedule_screen.cpp`: a five-day calendar with the open cups, the cup's text with P and,
  on choosing one, the question and the choice to enter. `TournamentAdapter::openSchedule` builds the calendar
  (`buildSchedule`, `cupOpen`) and `TournamentAdapter::enter` leaves the registration (triggers and variables 2 to 4).
- **Matches**: `apps/game/src/story_tournament.cpp` plays the three matches as `Tournament` battles with a clock
  (`BattleSetup::timeLimit`): no running away, no bag, no prizes or care at the end. They are played in the arena
  (`arena` section of the ruleset): `FieldScene` draws its model with its own camera (`FieldStage::view`,
  `render::MapRenderer::setStageCamera`) and the battle overrides its grid (`FieldStage::ground`).
- **Bracket**: `game::tournament::BracketAnimation` is the original's bracket frame by frame (20 per second): the
  presentation with the grade card, the board before each match and the champion's climb. It is drawn by
  `apps/game/src/tournament_bracket_screen.cpp` in the `ui/tournament_bracket` document with the images extracted from
  the player's own copy (`bracket` section of the ruleset and `data/stadium: bracket`). Without those tables the cup is
  played without a bracket.
- **Finish**: `TournamentAdapter::finish` removes the registration (trigger 37), adds to the record (cups won, lost,
  wins), sets the won-cup trigger (15 + cup), gives the ruleset's extra prizes (`extra_prizes`) and leaves the wins in
  variable 255, which the script reads (its prizes are in script 214).
- **Medal**: routine 22 (`TournamentAdapter::awardMedal`, `medal` section) gives the tournament medal and its line.

## Tests

`dwe_tournament_tests`: the bracket (tables, cells, presentation, matches with shots, the champion's climb), the
`bracket`, `extra_prizes` and `medal` sections with the tournament medal, a cup's lists in a script, the registration
calendar (open cups, five days, the 10:00 cutoff, entry day), the arena (which one, where the fighters start, the
camera), the rival draw, the bracket, the stand-ins, a rival's stats and techniques and the wins that take each cup;
`match_show_tests`, what is seen around the match (presentation, cameras, time out, letters), and
`champion_show_tests`, the champion scene (fades, letters and their gleam, the podium's lift, the camera's orbit and
rise). `dwe_battle_tests` (`tournament_match_tests.cpp`): the cup match without prizes, the defeat when the partner
falls, the clock, the judgment when time runs out, the hit chance, the wait of the group's techniques, the stance of the
one that disobeys and the end of confusion.

Route: `tests/autotest/route-stage3-tournaments.yaml` (en and es) enters cup C and loses on time, and in D the next day
and wins it (a win on time) up to the champion scene (`docs/guides/vertical-slice.md`).
